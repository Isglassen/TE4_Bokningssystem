The School

NTI Södertörn was founded in 2001 but back then it was under a different name, IT-Gymnasiet Södertörn. Back then it was owned by IT-Gymnasiet Sverige AB but in 2018 IT-Gymnasiet and NTI-Gymnasiet merged together. All of the IT-Gymnasie schools got their name changed to NTI-Gymnasiet
